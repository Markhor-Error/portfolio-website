Money

Affiliate Disclosure

Going Eagle uses affiliate links. Here is precisely what that means, what it costs you, and the rules we have set so it cannot bend our advice.

The plain version

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you click one and buy something, the retailer pays us a small commission. You pay exactly the same price either way — the commission comes out of the retailer's margin, not your pocket.

That is how this site is funded. There is no paywall, no subscription and no sponsored content, and we would rather be honest about the trade-off than pretend the content is free to produce.

What we will not do

These are commitments, not aspirations. If we break one, hold us to it.

  • We will not re-order a ranking for money. If the product paying us 3% is better than the one paying 10%, the 3% product goes first. Our testing methodology is published so you can check our reasoning against our conclusions.
  • We will not accept payment for a positive review. No brand can buy a score, a placement, or a favourable sentence on this site.
  • We will not hide the disclosure. It appears above the first affiliate link on the page, not only down here in the small print.
  • We will not replace a citation with an affiliate link. When a claim needs a source, it gets a source, even when there is a monetisable alternative.
  • We will not recommend a purchase you do not need. A significant number of pages here conclude that the reader should keep the equipment they own and spend the money on a lesson instead.

How to spot an affiliate link

Affiliate links on this site are marked in the page's HTML with rel="sponsored nofollow", which is the standard signal search engines and browser tools use to identify them. Every page containing one carries a visible notice above the first instance.

Programs we participate in

We work with golf retailers and manufacturers through affiliate networks. As our program memberships are confirmed we will list them here by name, so you can see exactly who has a financial relationship with this site.

Questions

If anything about our commercial arrangements is unclear, ask. Contact us and we will answer directly.
