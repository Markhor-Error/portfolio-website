# Competitor Intelligence — golferlogic.com

**Phase 1, competitor #2. Date: 2026-09-03.**

Same access constraint as competitor #1: `golferlogic.com` is blocked by the network egress
proxy, so this is reconstructed from indexed URLs, title tags and indexed excerpts. Structure,
titles and topic coverage are high-confidence. Depth, schema, internal links and performance
are unverified.

## 1. Positioning

"Golfer Logic." Instruction-led, with a distinctly opinionated technical voice — they build
swing content around the **Manuel de la Torre** method (swing the clubhead, let the body
respond) rather than generic body-rotation instruction. They also review **coaches** as
products, which neither we nor Going Low currently do.

Observed hubs: `/golf-tips/`, `/golf-clubs/`. Articles are flat at root, same as Going Low.

Note also that this cluster is the *only* part of Golfer Logic refreshed to 2026. They know
where their revenue is. We should read that as confirmation, not coincidence.

**Clubs (`/golf-clubs/`):** `/golf-club-brands/`, `/best-wedges-high-handicappers/`,
best drivers for high handicappers, best driving irons, used-club buying guides, Callaway

**Home golf:** as tabled above.

**Training aids:** `/best-golf-training-aids/` (2024), `/alignment-sticks/` (2023).

## 4. Their weaknesses

1. **Severe title-tag decay outside the simulator cluster.** In late 2026 they are still
   publishing "The 10 Best Online Golf Coaches In **2023**", "best alignment sticks … in
   **2023**", "6 Best Indoor Golf Putting Greens for **2023**", "The 10 Best Golf Club Brands
   In **2023**", "GCQuad: **2023** price", training aids "in **2024**". Roughly half the
   indexed library visibly advertises itself as three years stale.
2. **Two hubs for a large library.** `/golf-tips/` and `/golf-clubs/` cannot organise the
   simulator content, the coaching reviews, or the practice content. The home-golf cluster —
   their most valuable asset — appears to have **no hub at all**.
3. **Flat URLs, no topical path.** Same weakness as Going Low.
4. **Narrow instruction philosophy.** Committing to one swing method is a real voice, but it
   limits which queries they can credibly serve.
5. **No interactive tools observed** — same gap as Going Low.

## 5. Overlap between the two competitors (the contested middle)

Both cover: best drivers for slicers · training aids · alignment sticks · Callaway Edge
review · wedges for high handicappers · the shanks · beginner club sets · training-aid
roundups.

Contested topics are still worth entering, but only with a real differentiator — a tool,
original test data, or a materially better structure. They are never Tier 1 on their own.

## 7. Strategic conclusion

Two competent affiliate sites with the *same three structural flaws*: disordered taxonomy,
flat URLs, and zero interactive utility. Neither is beatable on volume; both are beatable on
**structure, tools, freshness discipline and trust**.

The single most important adjustment to our plan: **add a dedicated Home Golf cluster.**
Golfer Logic has proven the commercial value of that territory, and Going Low has left it open.
