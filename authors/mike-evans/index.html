About Mike

Mike Evans is an experienced golf coach with six years of hands-on coaching experience, helping golfers develop better technique, make smarter decisions on the course, and build a stronger understanding of the game. That practical coaching experience gives Going Eagle's golf content an additional layer of real-world expertise and scrutiny.

How Mike contributes to Going Eagle

Going Eagle's golf articles are researched and written by the Going Eagle Editorial Team, while Mike serves as an expert reviewer. Before publication, Mike reviews relevant golf content for accuracy, practical usefulness, and soundness of the advice. He provides feedback where needed and approves the final content before it goes live.

This editorial process lets us combine editorial research and writing with practical golf expertise, so readers can distinguish between general information and advice that has been reviewed by someone with substantial coaching experience.

Playing background

Mike has years of practical experience playing and coaching golf. We do not currently publish a specific handicap index, because we do not have a verified handicap figure to substantiate. If that changes, we will publish the figure and say where it came from.

Editorial transparency

We believe credibility comes from being transparent about who writes our content, who reviews it, and what qualifications those reviewers actually hold. Mike's role is therefore presented accurately: the Going Eagle Editorial Team writes the articles, and Mike Evans independently reviews and approves the golf content before publication.
