How this site works

Editorial Policy

Credibility comes from being transparent about who writes the content, who reviews it, and what those people are actually qualified to say. Here is exactly how Going Eagle works.

Who writes, who reviews

Articles on Going Eagle are researched and written by the Going Eagle Editorial Team. Golf content is then reviewed before publication by Mike Evans, a golf coach with six years of coaching experience. Mike checks the advice for accuracy, practical usefulness and soundness, gives feedback where it is needed, and approves the final version before it goes live.

We keep those two roles separate and label them separately on every article. Mike is a reviewer, not a bylined author, and we will never present him as having written something he did not write.

What the review badge means

When you see “Reviewed by Mike Evans, Golf Coach” on an article, it means that specific article was read and approved by Mike before publication. Pages without that badge have not been through coach review yet — usually because they are reference or administrative pages rather than golf advice.

Rules we hold ourselves to

  • No invented credentials. We publish the qualifications our reviewers actually hold and nothing more. We do not publish a handicap index for Mike because we do not have a verified figure to substantiate.
  • No stock or AI-generated portraits of real people. Where we do not yet have a genuine photograph, we use a plain monogram and say so.
  • No fabricated testing. If we have not physically tested a product, the page says the recommendation is research-based. See how we test.
  • No invented statistics. Where we cite a figure we say where it came from. Where a reliable figure does not exist, we say that instead of estimating one.
  • Commission never changes a ranking. If the product paying us less is the better product, it is listed first. See our affiliate disclosure.
  • Illustrations are labelled. Diagrams and illustrative images are our own. We never present an illustration as a photograph of a real product or event.

Keeping content current

Golf advice ages at different speeds. A definition of a stroke index will be true in ten years; a list of the best launch monitors will be wrong within one. Every page on this site is assigned a review schedule when it is written:

Type of contentReviewed everyExamples
Fast-moving3 monthsProduct recommendations, prices, rankings, rules changes
Moderate6 monthsComparisons, buying guides, anything tied to a specific tool
Stable12 monthsDefinitions, technique fundamentals, background explanations

We change the “last updated” date on an article only when the content genuinely changed. Refreshing a date to look current, without touching the substance, is a practice we will not use.

Corrections

If something here is wrong, we want to know. Tell us through the contact page and we will correct it. Substantive corrections are noted on the page itself rather than quietly edited away.
