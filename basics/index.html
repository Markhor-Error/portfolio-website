Golf Basics

Golf Basics: The Game Explained Without Condescension

Golf has an unusual amount of assumed knowledge, and an unhelpful culture around admitting you do not have it. Nothing here assumes you already know the answer.

ME

Written by Going Eagle Editorial Team · Reviewed by Mike Evans, Golf Coach — 6 years of coaching experience

What actually stops new golfers

It is rarely the swing. It is not knowing where to stand, when to walk, what to write on the card, or what to do when the ball goes somewhere the rules have a specific opinion about. That uncertainty is what makes the first few rounds stressful, and it is entirely fixable in an afternoon of reading.

The only three rules you need on day one

Play the ball as it lies. Count every stroke, including the ones you would rather not. Do not hold anybody up. Everything else can be learned as it comes up, and nobody decent will mind.

Where to go next

If you are brand new, read the scoring explainer first — it makes everything else make sense. If you have played a while but never got round to asking what a stroke index column is for, the handicap guide is the one.
