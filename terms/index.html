Terms of Use

Plain terms for a content site. Nothing unusual, but worth stating.

What this site is

Going Eagle publishes golf instruction, equipment guidance and free calculators. It is general information, not personal coaching. Golf technique is individual, and advice that helps one player can hurt another — which is why we recommend a qualified coach for anything that matters to you.

The calculators

Our tools produce estimates using published methods. The handicap calculator in particular is not an official handicap index and does not apply every adjustment a golf association would. Do not use it for competition purposes. Each tool states its own limits on its page.

Injury and physical risk

Golf swings put load on the back, wrists and elbows. If you have an injury or a medical condition, take advice from a qualified professional before changing your technique or your practice volume.

Accuracy

We work hard to be accurate and we correct mistakes when we find them or when readers report them. We cannot guarantee that every detail is current at the moment you read it, particularly for prices and product availability. Our editorial policy describes how we keep pages current.

Affiliate relationships

We earn commission on some purchases made through our links, disclosed on every page that carries one. See the full disclosure.

Content ownership

The writing, diagrams and tools on this site are ours. Quote us with a link — do not republish pages wholesale.
